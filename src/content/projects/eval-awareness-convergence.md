## Question

Do different methods for measuring eval awareness agree?

## What I did

I run the chain-of-thought and verbalized-awareness conditions. Co-authors: Julius Šula, María Marino, Netzer Epstein and Neta Ravid.

## Where it stands

We are building a shared harness and a small "zero tolerance" dataset of prompts that are obviously an evaluation or obviously not. Every instrument has to separate those cleanly before we compare them on the ambiguous cases, where the interesting disagreements should show up.

## Why it matters

Papers report that a model "is eval-aware" some share of the time, but that number comes from one instrument. If a probe, the Jacobian lens and the model's own reasoning disagree, then the claim depends on which one you picked, and anyone correcting safety evals for awareness needs to know which instrument actually tracks behaviour.
